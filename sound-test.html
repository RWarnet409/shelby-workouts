Sound test for the ⏱ ding

Which way of making a sound works on this phone: with the ring/silent switch on silent, and without stopping the music.

How to run it
  1. Start music in Spotify (AirPods or speaker). Flip the side switch to silent.
  2. Come back here and tap each button once. Each one should sound twice: right away, and again 5 seconds later (that second one is what the ⏱ clock would do). Keep this page on screen for those 5 seconds.
  3. For each button note: heard it? Did the music keep going, dip for a moment, or stop?
  4. Flip the switch to ring and tap them all again.
  5. Optional: Share → Add to Home Screen, open it from the icon, and repeat. That is how Shelby runs the app.

If the results start to look mixed up, reload the page between buttons.
